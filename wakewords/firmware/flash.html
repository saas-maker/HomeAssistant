VivaJot — Voice PE Firmware Flasher

Installs the VivaJot firmware with the "VivaJot Help" and "Help Help" wake words onto a Home Assistant Voice PE speaker.

  1. Plug the speaker into this computer with a USB-C data cable.
  2. Use Chrome or Edge (Safari and Firefox can't flash).
  3. Click Connect, pick the device's serial port, then Install.

After first boot, add the speaker in the Home Assistant app (it appears as a new device), then pick the wake word on its device page. Future firmware updates arrive over Wi-Fi automatically — this page is only needed for new speakers.
